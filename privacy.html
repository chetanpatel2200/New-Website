Privacy Policy

How Bhoomi Investment collects, uses and protects your personal information.

Last updated: September 2026

Bhoomi Investment ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through bhoomiinvestment.org and in the course of providing our services, in line with applicable Indian law including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, phone number, email address, preferred appointment details and any message you submit through our forms.
  • Client information: for clients, KYC details and financial information required by regulators, asset management companies and insurers to process transactions.
  • Technical information: basic, non-identifying data such as browser type and pages visited, which may be collected by our hosting provider.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and schedule appointments.
  • To provide financial planning, investment distribution, insurance and related services.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and KYC obligations.
  • To send service updates and, with your consent, relevant information about our services.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where necessary with mutual fund houses, registrars (RTAs), insurers, lenders, KYC registration agencies and technology providers who help us deliver our services, or where required by law.

4. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Retention

We keep personal information only as long as required for the purposes above or as required by applicable regulations.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information (subject to regulatory retention requirements), and you may withdraw consent for marketing communication at any time by contacting us.

7. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party websites such as client portals, maps and messaging services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
