FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a large amount to start investing?

Not at all. You can start a SIP in many mutual funds with a small monthly amount and increase it as your income grows. What matters most is starting early and staying disciplined.

How do I meet your team?

We work by appointment so we can give you our full attention. Use the appointment form, call us on or visit our office in Idar after booking a time.

Do you offer both investments and insurance?

Yes. We help you with mutual funds, alternate investments, fixed deposits and bonds, as well as life, health and motor insurance — so your growth and protection plans work together.

Can I track my investments online?

Yes. Existing clients can request access to our client portal to view their portfolio and statements.

How often will my portfolio be reviewed?

We recommend at least one comprehensive review every year and whenever there is a major life event — marriage, a new child, a job change, or a large purchase.
